Q2 2026 regression and trend investigation

Scope: The latest complete quarter in the available data is Q2 2026 (April–June). July has only a few partial rows and was excluded.

Executive finding

The strongest regression is passport capture quality. The share of document uploads crossing the failed-attempt threshold rose from 9.6% in April to 13.1% in May and 17.7% in June—an increase of 8.1 percentage points, or roughly 1.8x April's rate.

Application starts increased 15.6% from April to June, but purchases declined 6.8%. From May to June, starts increased 2.4% while purchasers fell 9.8%; purchase value fell 7.2%. The monthly purchaser-to-start ratio was approximately 4.9% in April, 4.5% in May, and 4.0% in June. Because event timestamps are not reliably ordered across later funnel stages, this should be treated as a directional monthly rate rather than a strict cohort conversion rate.

Android pay-now users fell from 633 in April to 487 in June, while Android purchasers fell from 305 to 223. This is consistent with the Android capture regression being a meaningful contributor to the June funnel weakness.

Retry and app-version checks

The threshold regression is not limited to high-retry users. For retry_count 0, the rate rose from 9.6% in April to 18.3% in June; retry_count 1 rose from 10.8% to 17.2%; retry_count 2 rose from 7.6% to 14.7%. This suggests a broad capture-quality or environment issue rather than only a tail of repeated retries.

All five app versions—7.42.0, 7.43.1, 7.44.0, 7.45.2, and 7.46.0—showed roughly the same movement: approximately 8–11% in April, 12–14% in May, and 17–19% in June. There is no clear version-specific rollout curve.

Other operational areas

Abandonment recovery, express checkout, forex add-on, and visa-status sharing records are concentrated in June in the current dataset. They can describe June operations but cannot support a reliable last-quarter regression comparison. Therefore no quarter trend is claimed for these areas.

Recommended follow-up

  1. Investigate the Android document-capture path, including camera permissions, capture mode, device model/browser/client-library, and image-quality changes introduced during May–June.
  2. Break the threshold regression down by citizenship and device subtype to distinguish traffic-mix effects from a platform defect.
  3. Validate whether June document uploads and purchases are fully represented before using the funnel decline for business reporting.
  4. Backfill at least April–May data for recovery, express checkout, forex, and sharing events before assessing their quarter trends.
