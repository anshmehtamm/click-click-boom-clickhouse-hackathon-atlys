Visa Status Sharing Analytics Report

Coverage: 2026-06-08 to 2026-07-01 UTC. Rows: 6,503. The canonical table is present as a SharedMergeTree and contains all five specified event types.

1. Table shape and data quality

  • Event volumes: 1,600 share_clicked; 1,144 channel_selected; 1,144 link_generated; 2,310 link_opened; 305 recipient_cta_clicked.
  • Identifiers: all 6,503 event_id values are unique; no empty event_id values or ingest_version values above 1 were observed.
  • Sharer envelope: share_clicked, channel_selected, and link_generated rows have one distinct user, application, and share per row.
  • Recipient envelope: link_opened and recipient_cta_clicked rows have blank status/channel/device dimensions and collapse to one user_id and one application_id. Attribution should therefore use share_id, not recipient user_id.
  • Destination: all rows have a populated destination; destination_key is suitable for grouping.

2. Share rate and status mix

A valid share rate requires an eligible sharer denominator, such as distinct applications or users that reached a shareable visa status. That denominator is not available in this table, and the 1,600 sharing application IDs did not match application_started.application_id in a direct join. Therefore, a true share rate cannot be computed.

For orientation only, 1,600 share clicks divided by 23,417 application_started rows during the same calendar window equals 6.83%. This is a rough volume ratio, not a valid share rate.

4. Recipient-to-application CTA conversion

At share grain, 771 generated links received at least one new-user open and 263 received at least one CTA, producing a raw share-level conversion of 34.1%. However, timestamp validation found only 182 shares where the first CTA occurred at or after the first new-user open. The preferred ordered conversion is therefore 182 / 771 = 23.6%.

5. Destinations that spread most

By new-user open volume, the leading destinations are AU (135 new-user open events; 72 opened shares), TH (112; 62), VN (107; 59), FR (104; 60), and AE (103; 54). AU has the most spread volume, while AE has the highest CTA-per-new-open-event ratio among the leading destinations at 27.2%, followed by FR at 25.0%, TH at 23.2%, and VN at 21.5%.

6. Recommendations and instrumentation gaps

  1. Add or reliably join an eligible-status application/user population so share rate can be measured by status_shared.
  2. Preserve channel and status on recipient events or maintain a documented share_id attribution join; current raw recipient rows have blank values.
  3. Instrument the downstream application_started event for recipients, keyed to share_id, to turn CTA conversion into true recipient-to-new-application conversion.
  4. Continue deduplicating by event_id before canonical insertion and before any materialized-view backfill.
  5. Use share_id-level metrics for opens and CTA conversion; do not interpret recipient user_id counts as production user counts until the synthetic envelope limitation is resolved.
