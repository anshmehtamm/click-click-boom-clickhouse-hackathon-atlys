Express Checkout analysis

Window: June 8–28, 2026. Grain: application_id-level conditional aggregates over the canonical express_checkout_events MergeTree table.

Executive conclusion

Express adoption is healthy: 1,007 of 1,650 eligible shown applications selected Express, a 61.0% adoption rate. There were 836 confirmed payments, equal to 50.7% shown-to-confirm conversion. The principal product risk is iOS OTP performance: iOS OTP success was 83.6%, versus 100% on Android, web, and Desktop.

Express payment processing itself is fast, with a roughly 2.3-second median. The roughly 10-minute shown-to-confirm median includes user waiting and OTP entry, not just processor latency. The standard timing comparison is directional only because its start event is pay-now rather than shown, event timestamps are known to be unreliable across later funnel stages, and only 462 non-negative standard intervals were usable.

Recommended actions

  1. Prioritize iOS OTP instrumentation and debugging, including autofill behavior, OTP delivery delay, retry counts, and app-version/device-browser combinations.
  2. Run a controlled Express-versus-standard experiment among equally eligible users to establish causal conversion lift.
  3. Instrument timestamps or client-side duration fields for shown, selected, OTP submitted, and confirmed so waiting time can be decomposed from payment-processor latency.
  4. Add destination, citizenship, acquisition, and app-version cuts to the recurring Express dashboard; preserve application_id-level deduplication and avoid sequence-based funnel functions past document upload.

Data-quality note: the table has one row per raw event and no materialized view. All funnel rates above were reconstructed with conditional aggregates grouped by application_id. Monetary fields are Int64 without a declared minor-unit convention and were not used for revenue comparisons.
